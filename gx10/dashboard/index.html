What the colours mean

Left: phone picture, upright, with YOLO boxes (amber) and hazards (violet). Right: what the LiDAR obstacle check sees. Green floor; obstacles red under 0.8 m, amber under 1.5 m (what a heading needs to be usable), yellow beyond; magenta too close to measure; dark blue no reading (both count as blocked); grey is beyond range or above the cane. Lines split the seven headings, each labelled with its clearance in metres. The side the wheel turns to, or the side you hold in manual steering, stays bright with its obstacles outlined.

How it works

Hold a button to spin the motor that way; let go and it stops. No set positions, and free space is not checked while you control it. Keys: ← counter-clockwise, → clockwise. If the directions come out reversed on the cane, start the Pi with --swap. If this page closes, the wheel goes back to automatic within a second.
